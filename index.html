Executive Overview

Understanding Offer Codes in Banking Context

What Are Offer Codes in Banking?

Simple Explanation: Offer codes are special "passwords" or "keys" that give customers rewards, discounts, or special benefits when they open accounts, refer friends, or use specific banking products. Think of them like coupons at a grocery store, but for bank accounts and services.

Refer-a-Friend & Direct Mail Campaigns

ARefer a Friend Program - Complete Implementation

What is "Refer a Friend" in Banking?

Your existing customer (Referrer) tells their friend about your bank. The friend (Referee) opens an account using a special code. Both get money or rewards. Example: "Give $50, Get $50" - When John refers Mary, and Mary opens a checking account with code JOHN2024, John gets $50 and Mary gets $50.

Step-by-Step Process in Dynamics 365:

1
Customer Logs In
Existing customer accesses referral portal via mobile app or online banking
2
System Generates Code
Dynamics 365 creates unique code: REF-[CustomerID]-[Random]
3
Friend Uses Code
Referee enters code during account opening application
4
Validation & Reward
System validates account opening and triggers reward payment

Dynamics 365 Entities & Configuration:

Component D365 Entity/Feature Configuration Details Banking Data Fields
Referrer Contact (Customer) Existing customer record with active accounts Customer ID, Account Number, Account Status, Referral Eligibility Flag
Referral Code Marketing Coupon (Custom) Auto-generated unique identifier Code Pattern: REF-XXXX-XXXX, Expiry Date (90 days), Max Uses: 5
Referee Lead → Contact New prospect converted to customer Referred By (Lookup), Referral Code Used, Application Date
Reward Custom Entity: Referral Reward Tracks who gets paid what and when Reward Amount ($50), Status (Pending/Paid), Payment Method, 1099 Flag
Account Opening Integration with Core Banking Real-time API to check account status Account Open Date, Account Type (DDA/Savings), Minimum Balance Met

Detailed Field Specifications:

Referral Code Generation Logic
Format: REF-[Last4OfAccount]-[Random4Digits]
Example: REF-7890-4521
Uniqueness: Guaranteed unique via Dataverse duplicate detection rule
Expiration: 90 days from generation (configurable)
Usage Limit: Maximum 5 successful referrals per customer per quarter (configurable)
Character Set: Uppercase letters (excluding I, O, 1, 0 to avoid confusion) + Numbers
Validation Rules (OOTB + Custom)
Referrer Validation:
• Account must be open > 90 days
• Account status = Active (not dormant, not closed)
• No fraud flags
• Not employed by the bank (REG O check)

Referee Validation:
• New customer (SSN/TIN not in system)
• Age 18+
• Not on OFAC/sanctions list
• Meets account opening requirements (ChexSystems clear)
Reward Fulfillment Conditions
Trigger Events:
1. Referee account opened
2. Initial deposit received (minimum $25)
3. Account remains open for 30 days
4. No early closure (chargeback if closed < 90 days)

Payment Methods:
• Direct deposit to referrer's DDA
• Statement credit
• Check mailed (for closed accounts)
• 1099-INT or 1099-MISC generation for tax reporting

Regulatory Compliance Requirements

Truth in Savings (Reg DD): Must disclose bonus/interest as APY if it affects rate
Unfair or Deceptive Acts (UDAAP): Cannot advertise "free money" without conditions
Bank Secrecy Act: Monitor for structuring (multiple small referrals)
Tax Reporting: $600+ in rewards requires 1099-MISC
Privacy: Cannot share referrer info with referee without consent

BMailing Codes (Direct Mail Campaigns)

What are Mailing Codes?

Your bank sends physical letters to potential customers (prospects) with a special code. If they bring this letter (or enter the code online) to open an account, they get a cash bonus or waived fees. Example: "Bring this letter to any branch and use code MAIL500 to get $200 when you open a new checking account."

Complete Process Flow:

2
Code Assignment

3
Letter Print & Mail

4

BNON-DDA Campaign Offer Codes

What is NON-DDA?

DDA = Demand Deposit Account (checking/savings). NON-DDA means everything else: Certificates of Deposit (CDs), Individual Retirement Accounts (IRAs), Investment accounts, Money Market accounts. These often have different promotional rules because they're regulated differently (SEC vs. banking regulators).

NON-DDA Product Offer Codes:

Product Offer Type Code Example Regulatory Note
CD (Certificate of Deposit) Bonus Rate CDBONUS-5-25 Must disclose early withdrawal penalties (Reg DD)
IRA (Traditional/Roth) Transfer Bonus IRA-ROLLOVER Cannot guarantee investment performance (SEC)
Money Market Promo Rate MM-PROMO-4 Limited transactions (Reg D - 6/month)
Investment Account Free Trades TRADE-FREE-100 FINRA suitability requirements
Health Savings Inv. Investment Waiver HSA-INV-WAIVE IRS contribution limits apply

Critical Regulatory Distinction

Banking Products (DDA/CD/MM): Regulated by OCC/FDIC/Federal Reserve. Offers must comply with Truth in Savings.

Investment Products (IRA/Brokerage): Regulated by SEC/FINRA. Offers are "sales incentives" not "interest." Cannot advertise guaranteed returns. Requires suitability documentation.

CPositive Pay Campaign Offer Codes

What is Positive Pay?

A fraud prevention service for business checking accounts. The business sends a list of checks they've written to the bank. If someone tries to cash a check not on the list, the bank rejects it. Offer codes might waive setup fees or monthly fees for the first 3 months.

Positive Pay Offer Structure:

Offer Component Standard Pricing Promotional Offer Code Example
Setup Fee $250 Waived PP-SETUP-WAIVE
Monthly Fee $50/month 3 months free PP-3MO-FREE
Per Item Fee $0.10 per check First 1000 free/month PP-1K-FREE
Treasury Package $500/month bundle 50% off first year TREAS-50OFF-Y1

Treasury Management Integration:

Positive Pay is part of Treasury Management services. Dynamics 365 needs to integrate with:

  • Treasury Workstation: The system businesses use to upload check files
  • ACH Origination: For electronic payment fraud prevention (ACH Positive Pay)
  • Payee Positive Pay: Matches payee name, not just check number (higher security)
  • Exception Processing: Workflow for when checks don't match (pay or return decision)
// Positive Pay Offer Redemption Process WHEN BusinessCustomer.RedeemCode("PP-3MO-FREE") DO: // Validate business qualifies IF Account.Type = "Business Checking" AND Account.AverageBalance > $10000 THEN // Create Treasury Service record Service = NEW TreasuryService() Service.Type = "Positive Pay" Service.SetupFee = 0 // Waived Service.MonthlyFee = 0 // Promo period Service.PromoEndDate = TODAY() + 90 days Service.FullMonthlyFee = $50 // Schedule fee transition ScheduleTask("ActivateFullFee", Service.PromoEndDate) // Notify treasury management team SendNotification("Treasury Onboarding", Service) END IF END WHEN

DMerchant Services Campaign Offer Codes

Merchant Services Explained

When a business accepts credit cards (Visa, Mastercard), they need a merchant account. Your bank provides the account + card processing equipment. Offer codes might give free terminal, lower processing rates, or cash bonus for switching from another processor.

Merchant Services Offer Types:

Offer Code Value Conditions
Free Terminal MERCH-FREE-TERM $300 value Process $10K/month for 12 months
Rate Match MERCH-MATCH Beat current rate by 0.10% Provide current processor statement
Switch Bonus MERCH-SWITCH-500 $500 cash Switch from competitor, process $20K first month
No Monthly Minimum MERCH-NO-MIN $25/month waived First 6 months
Next Day Funding MERCH-ND-FUND Free upgrade Normally $10/month

Card Brand Compliance

Visa/Mastercard Rules: Cannot offer cash incentives to switch exclusively (must be for new business too). Must disclose all fees (interchange + markup). Dynamics 365 must track that offers comply with card brand rules to avoid fines.

EHealth Savings Account (HSA) Campaign Offer Codes

HSA Explained Simply

A Health Savings Account is a special bank account for people with high-deductible health insurance. Money goes in tax-free, grows tax-free, and comes out tax-free for medical expenses. Banks offer cash bonuses to open HSA accounts because they tend to have high balances and low turnover.

HSA Specific Offer Codes:

Offer Code Details IRS Compliance
Opening Bonus HSA-OPEN-100 $100 after first payroll deposit Does not count toward annual contribution limit
Balance Bonus HSA-BAL-500 $50 for every $1000 maintained (max $250) Must be reported on Form 1099-SA if distributed
Investment Waiver HSA-INV-FREE Waive $2.50/month investment fee Fee reduction is not a contribution
Employer Bundle HSA-EMP-BUNDLE Free for employer groups >100 employees Employer contributions tracked separately

HSA Eligibility Verification (Critical):

Before allowing HSA offer redemption, Dynamics 365 must verify:

  1. High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP): Minimum deductible $1,650 individual / $3,300 family (2025 limits)
  2. No Other Coverage: Not enrolled in Medicare, FSA, or other non-HDHP
  3. Not Claimed Dependent: Cannot be claimed on someone else's tax return
  4. Contribution Limit Check: Ensure opening bonus + customer contribution doesn't exceed $4,300 (individual) or $8,550 (family) for 2025

HSA + Investment Integration

Many HSAs allow investment once balance exceeds $1,000. Dynamics 365 should track:
• Cash balance (for debit card)
• Investment balance (mutual funds)
• Total balance for fee waiver qualification
• Investment risk tolerance (conservative/aggressive)

Pre-Approved Credit Cards

Reservation Numbers & Soft Pull Campaigns

1Pre-Approved Offer Mechanics

What Does "Pre-Approved" Mean?

The bank already did a "soft pull" (doesn't affect credit score) and determined the customer likely qualifies. They send a letter/email saying "You're pre-approved for up to $10,000 credit limit!" with a reservation number. When customer applies with that number, they get the offer terms (if information hasn't changed).

Complete Pre-Approval Process:

1
Soft Pull
Bank requests credit bureau data (Experian, Equifax, TransUnion) without hard inquiry
2
Scoring Model
Internal algorithm determines pre-approval likelihood and credit limit
3
Offer Generation
Dynamics 365 creates personalized offer with reservation number
4
Customer Response
Customer applies online/phone with reservation number
5
Hard Pull & Verify
Hard credit inquiry + income verification
6
Final Decision
Approve with pre-approved terms or counter-offer/deny

Reservation Number Structure:

Component Format Example Purpose
Campaign ID 3 chars PLT Platinum card campaign
Month/Year 4 digits 0225 February 2025
Unique ID 8 digits 78432901 Sequential unique identifier
Checksum 2 digits 45 Fraud prevention (Luhn algorithm)
FULL CODE PLT-0225-78432901-45

Firm Offer of Credit: Pre-approval must be honored if customer meets stated criteria
Opt-Out: Must honor consumer requests to stop pre-screened offers
Record Retention: Keep soft pull data for 25 months (E-Oscar requirement)

2Risk Management & Fraud Prevention

Fraud Scenarios & Mitigation:

Code Guessing Bot tries random codes to find valid ones Rate limiting, Pattern analysis Max 3 attempts per IP, CAPTCHA after failure
Self-Referral Customer refers themselves using different email Device fingerprinting, Address matching Block same IP/device, Flag matching addresses

Clawback Mechanism

Dynamics 365 must support reversing rewards if fraud detected or conditions not met:
• Automatic: If account closed < 90 days, auto-debit reward from final balance
• Manual: Compliance officer can initiate clawback for fraud
• 1099 Correction: If clawback in same tax year, adjust 1099. If next year, customer reports recovery.
